<section class="home-hero">
  <p class="eyebrow">Information Systems · Operations Management · Urban Analytics</p>
  <h2>Data-driven research on digital infrastructure, human mobility, and intelligent decision systems.</h2>
  <p>
    I am a Ph.D. Candidate, expected to graduate in September 2026, jointly affiliated with the
    <strong>Department of Information Systems at City University of Hong Kong</strong> and the
    <strong>Department of Management Science at Xi'an Jiaotong University</strong>.
  </p>
</section>

My research examines the disruptive impacts of IT innovations on individuals, organizations, and urban systems, with a parallel interest in the design of data-driven decision systems. I combine causal inference, structural modeling, optimization algorithms, and large-scale behavioral data to generate evidence for both business leaders and policymakers.

<div class="research-compass">
  <article>
    <span class="compass-index">01</span>
    <h3>Urban Transportation Spillovers and Network Design</h3>
    <p>
      I study how bike-sharing and other urban mobility infrastructure reshape local commerce,
      taxi markets, crime, traffic safety, and welfare, then connect these findings to network
      design and resource allocation.
    </p>
  </article>
  <article>
    <span class="compass-index">02</span>
    <h3>Human Mobility Analysis and Application</h3>
    <p>
      I leverage population-scale GPS trajectory data to model spatiotemporal behavior and
      extend mobility-based analysis to urban business optimization and sequential decision contexts.
    </p>
  </article>
  <article>
    <span class="compass-index">03</span>
    <h3>LLM-Driven Operations Management Optimization</h3>
    <p>
      I design multi-agent LLM systems that translate latent shopping intent into personalized
      product portfolios, persuasion strategies, and domain knowledge structures.
    </p>
  </article>
</div>
