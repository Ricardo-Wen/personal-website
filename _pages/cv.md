## Research Interests

My research examines the disruptive impacts of IT innovations on individuals, organizations, and urban systems, and the design of data-driven decision systems. I employ causal inference, structural modeling, and optimization algorithms, integrating theories and methods from economics, management, and computer science.

- **Urban Transportation Spillovers and Network Design:** socioeconomic spillovers of bike-sharing and urban transportation infrastructure on mobility, local business outcomes, taxi markets, crime, traffic safety, and welfare.
- **Human Mobility Analysis and Application:** population-scale GPS trajectory data for modeling individual spatiotemporal behavior and extending trajectory-based analysis to urban business and sequential decision contexts.
- **LLM-Driven Operations Management Optimization:** multi-agent LLM architectures for intent-driven recommendation, personalized persuasion, domain knowledge organization, and latent insight extraction.

## In-Progress Projects

- **Reshaping Urban Consumption Geography:** a deep structural choice model of bike-sharing infrastructure and spatial consideration sets, with Junming Liu and Natasha Zhang Foutze.
- **From Fear to Footprints:** tracing the behavioral impact of urban violence with GPS trajectory data, with Junming Liu and Natasha Zhang Foutze.
- **Optimal Product Sequencing in Livestream Commerce:** a structural model of cognitive resource dynamics, with Baozhen Zhan and Yulan (Amanda) Wang.
- **From Products to Solutions:** designing a multi-agent LLM system for intent-driven grocery recommendation, with Junming Liu.
- **Model-Free Online Learning for Revenue Management under Censored Demand:** an entropy-regularized online optimization framework, with Junming Liu and Yimin Yu.
